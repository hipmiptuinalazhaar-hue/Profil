# Design System Foundation

## Personality ratio
- 60% premium institutional
- 20% entrepreneurial
- 10% editorial
- 10% experimental technology

## Color architecture
- Ink 950 `#06101D`: deepest surfaces.
- Ink 900 `#07111F`: primary institutional navy.
- Ink 800 `#0D1A2A`: elevated dark surface.
- Paper 50 `#F7F7F2`: primary light canvas.
- Paper 100 `#EEEFE8`: secondary neutral.
- Gold 500 `#C7A15A`: controlled brand accent.
- Gold 600 `#AA8241`: active/hover accent.
- Green 600 `#0F6A51`: secondary institutional signal.

Gold is an accent, never a wallpaper.

## Typography
- Display: Instrument Serif. Used for major editorial statements and section anchors.
- Body/UI: Manrope. Used for navigation, body copy, metadata, forms, and data.

No generic SaaS typography rhythm. Headlines should create hierarchy, not merely become larger body text.

## Radius
- Small: 8px
- Medium: 14px
- Large: 24px

Avoid excessive pill shapes. Geometry should feel architectural rather than playful.

## Motion principles
Motion may clarify hierarchy, reveal imagery, or signal state. It must not exist only to prove that JavaScript loaded.

Preferred: clip reveals, restrained parallax, staggered type, number transitions, page transitions.
Avoid: endless floating, bounce loops, gratuitous glow, cursor gimmicks.

## Responsive rule
Design from 360px upward. Editorial asymmetry may appear on large screens but content order must remain logical on mobile.
