# Phase 1 — International Benchmark Matrix

This matrix defines what HIPMI PT should learn from leading global organizations without visually cloning them.

## 1. Endeavor
Website: `https://endeavor.org/`

### Why it matters
Endeavor presents entrepreneurship as an ecosystem rather than a collection of events. Its public experience connects founders, mentors, investment, global community, success stories, and measurable impact.

### Patterns to adopt
- ecosystem-first storytelling
- entrepreneur/member stories as proof
- mentor/network/business relationships shown as one system
- strong narrative movement from ambition to scale to multiplier effect
- impact used as evidence, not decoration
- editorial use of real founder/company photography

### What not to copy
- global scale claims
- corporate visual details specific to Endeavor
- investor/fund structures HIPMI PT does not yet operate

### HIPMI PT translation
Use the same systemic logic for:
`Student → Member → Learning → Business → Market → Mentorship → Network → Capital → Growth`

Weight in our design DNA: **40%**.

---

## 2. YPO
Website: `https://www.ypo.org/`

### Why it matters
YPO communicates exclusivity and leadership quality with minimal messaging, strong photography, confident typography, and a clear membership proposition.

### Patterns to adopt
- leadership as a premium identity asset
- concise high-confidence hero messaging
- whitespace and large-scale typography
- membership presented as a valuable journey
- relationship/network value explained emotionally and practically

### What not to copy
- CEO exclusivity language
- age/qualification model
- luxury-club tone that would alienate students

### HIPMI PT translation
Leadership should feel credible and aspirational without becoming elitist. Ketua, Sekretaris, Bendahara, and six Kabid must be treated as real institutional figures with complete photo coverage.

Weight in our design DNA: **20%**.

---

## 3. Junior Chamber International (JCI)
Website: `https://jci.cc/`

### Why it matters
JCI is structurally the closest global reference: young leaders, business/entrepreneurship, training, events, international/local networks, impact, membership, and organizational legitimacy.

### Patterns to adopt
- clear areas of opportunity
- programs tied to member development
- local action connected to wider networks
- events as development/networking opportunities
- impact report mindset
- strong join/get involved pathway

### What not to copy
- generic nonprofit visual patterns
- global chapter structures that do not match HIPMI PT governance

### HIPMI PT translation
Programs, membership, organization, events, and impact need a clear information architecture so visitors understand both what HIPMI PT does and how they participate.

Weight in our design DNA: **20%**.

---

## 4. World Economic Forum
Website: `https://www.weforum.org/`

### Why it matters
WEF demonstrates institutional authority through content hierarchy, issue/centre structures, stakeholder framing, insight density, and partnership language.

### Patterns to adopt
- strong institutional headline hierarchy
- impact/stakeholder framing
- dense information presented without visual chaos
- editorial authority for reports, insights, and public affairs
- partnership language that sounds serious rather than promotional

### What not to copy
- global-policy complexity
- dense mega-navigation at HIPMI PT’s current scale
- visual severity that would make the site feel bureaucratic

### HIPMI PT translation
Use WEF-style institutional discipline for Impact, Partnership, Insights, government collaboration, and future reports.

Weight in our design DNA: **10%**.

---

## 5. TED
Website: `https://www.ted.com/about`

### Why it matters
TED is excellent at turning real people, events, programs, and ideas into editorial narratives that are easy to browse and emotionally memorable.

### Patterns to adopt
- content-first storytelling
- documentary images as meaningful evidence
- stories tied to mission and action
- programs grouped by what they achieve
- impact explained through human and institutional outcomes

### What not to copy
- media-platform behavior unrelated to HIPMI PT
- entertainment-style density
- talk/video-first architecture

### HIPMI PT translation
Pelantikan, BPC mentorship, UMKM bazaar, government meetings, business visits, and future events become photo essays/stories rather than gallery leftovers.

Weight in our design DNA: **10%**.

# Combined design equation

HIPMI PT V2 is not:

`Endeavor + YPO + JCI + WEF + TED visually mixed together`

It is:

`Endeavor ecosystem logic`
`+ YPO leadership prestige`
`+ JCI organizational/member architecture`
`+ WEF institutional authority`
`+ TED documentary storytelling`
`+ HIPMI PT identity, photography, programs, people, location, and culture`

## Benchmark test
Before implementation approval, ask:

1. Does the page explain a real system, or only decorate information?
2. Does photography prove activity and leadership?
3. Is the organization understandable without prior HIPMI knowledge?
4. Would a rector, mayor, entrepreneur, student, and potential partner all find a meaningful path?
5. Is the final visual identity still unmistakably HIPMI PT UIN Al Azhaar, not a copy of a benchmark?

If the fifth answer is no, the benchmark has been used incorrectly.
