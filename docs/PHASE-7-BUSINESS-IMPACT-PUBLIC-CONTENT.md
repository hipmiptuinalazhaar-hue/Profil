# Phase 7 — Business, Impact & Public Content

Status: implemented on `phase-6-9-complete-platform`.

## Goal
Expand the website from an institutional/program platform into a truthful public-facing headquarters for business development, impact evidence, events, insights, and documentation.

## Delivered
- `/business`: venture-development architecture and a verification-first member business directory state.
- `/impact`: verified organizational baseline, evidence standards, and a four-stage measurement framework.
- `/events`: public calendar architecture that refuses to invent dates or registrations.
- `/insights`: three practical public playbooks on venture validation, pitching, and networking.
- `/media`: repository-backed official documentation collections with canonical photo filenames.
- Homepage public-platform gateway connecting the major public areas.

## Evidence policy
Targets, planned programs, activities, outputs, and outcomes remain distinct. No invented revenue, participant counts, partner claims, future dates, or impact statistics are used to fill empty space.

## Media policy
Missing documentation remains an explicit filename placeholder until the corresponding image is uploaded to `public/photos`.
