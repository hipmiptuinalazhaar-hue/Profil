# Phase 1 — Creative Direction & Design Blueprint

Status: **complete**

Phase 1 exists to prevent premature production coding. Its output is a design intelligence system detailed enough that later implementation does not default to generic UI patterns.

## Creative thesis

**Entrepreneurial Institution, Presented Like a National Editorial Brand.**

HIPMI PT UIN Al Azhaar Lubuklinggau should not look like:
- a generic student organization
- a SaaS landing page
- a government portal
- a startup template
- an AI-generated portfolio

It should feel like a serious young-entrepreneur institution with documentary credibility, business relevance, leadership prestige, and editorial confidence.

## Emotional target

Within the first 5–10 seconds, a visitor should feel:

1. This is a real organization.
2. These people actually meet, build, collaborate, and lead.
3. The organization is connected to business and public institutions.
4. The brand is young but not childish.
5. The experience is premium without pretending to be a luxury label.

## Primary audiences

### Student / prospective member
Needs to understand what HIPMI PT is, what opportunities exist, who leads it, and how to join.

### Entrepreneur / mentor / senior HIPMI network
Needs evidence of seriousness, programs, leadership, member/business potential, and collaboration quality.

### University leadership
Needs institutional clarity, organizational legitimacy, program alignment, and impact potential.

### Government / public institution
Needs credible positioning, documented activity, regional relevance, and clear partnership pathways.

### Company / investor / partner
Needs a clean view of ecosystem, programs, businesses, audience, leadership, and ways to collaborate.

## Benchmark allocation

Functional DNA, not visual cloning:

- **40% Endeavor** — entrepreneurial ecosystem, mentor/network/business narrative, multiplier effect.
- **20% YPO** — prestige, leadership, membership quality, high-value community framing.
- **20% JCI** — organization/member architecture, programs, events, global-local action.
- **10% World Economic Forum** — institutional authority, impact, stakeholders, partnership hierarchy.
- **10% TED** — documentary storytelling, editorial media, ideas-to-action presentation.

Detailed benchmark notes live in `PHASE-1-BENCHMARK-MATRIX.md`.

## Original HIPMI PT identity layer

The site must not become a collage of those references. HIPMI PT owns the final identity through:

- midnight navy institutional base
- restrained brass-gold accents
- warm paper/off-white editorial canvases
- authentic HIPMI PT photography
- organization-specific names, roles, programs, locations, and stories
- Lubuklinggau / Linggau Raya regional context
- 2026/2027 leadership identity
- bilingual Indonesian/English architecture
- visual hierarchy closer to an annual report + business magazine than a campus microsite

## Hero art direction

Working concept: **Institutional Momentum**.

The hero should feel like a live editorial cover built from real organization photography, not a centered text block with decorative cards.

### Message hierarchy

Institutional label:
`Official Digital Headquarters · HIPMI PT UIN Al Azhaar Lubuklinggau`

Primary headline:
`Membangun Generasi Pengusaha Muda Kampus.`

Manifesto strap:
`Build business. Grow network. Create impact.`

Supporting copy explains collaboration, learning, mentorship, networking, business development, and regional impact in 2–3 concise lines.

Primary CTA:
`Explore HIPMI PT`

Secondary CTA:
`Join Our Network`

### Composition

Desktop:
- 12-column grid
- headline spans approximately 7 columns
- documentary image composition occupies approximately 5–6 columns and may intentionally overlap grid boundaries
- one dominant image, not a slideshow
- optional two supporting documentary frames with strong cropping and low visual noise
- small metadata line: event / city / year

Mobile:
- content order: label → headline → supporting copy → CTA → dominant image
- no tiny floating photo collage
- image remains large enough to recognize faces and context

## Section design principles

### Institutional Statement
A strong transition out of the hero. Large type, generous whitespace, minimal decoration.

Suggested statement:
`Not another campus organization.`

Followed by a factual Indonesian explanation of HIPMI PT as an entrepreneur ecosystem.

### Impact Snapshot
No cards. Use a horizontal editorial metric field or oversized typographic composition.

Only verified facts are allowed, such as:
- 6 organizational fields
- 9 flagship programs
- period 2026/2027
- Linggau Raya network context

### About & Vision
Use vision as an editorial quotation / manifesto, paired with real documentary context or structured mission sequence.

### Programs
Nine programs become a numbered **Program Index**, not nine equal cards.

Example rhythm:
`01 Business Academy`
`02 Pitching & Access to Capital`
`03 Networking & Business Expo`
...

Desktop may reveal supporting description/media on focus/hover. Mobile becomes a clean vertical sequence.

### Entrepreneur Ecosystem
Signature section visualizing:

`Student → Member → Learning → Business → Market → Mentorship → Network → Capital → Growth`

HIPMI PT is the connector, not a decorative logo in the center of a meaningless web.

### Business Ecosystem
Explain the relationship between:
- Business Directory
- Pasar UMKM
- Business Academy
- mentorship
- partnership
- investment/finance literacy
- member growth

If business data is not yet verified, show a premium transparent empty state.

### Documentary Stories
Use photo-essay logic for real activities, including:
- Pelantikan Akbar HIPMI PT Kampus se-Linggau Raya 2026 + UMKM bazaar
- sharing/networking with senior entrepreneurs / BPC HIPMI Lubuklinggau
- strategic government collaboration with Wali Kota Lubuklinggau

Each story requires title, context, real photography, and meaning. No auto-rotating carousel.

### Impact
Institutional, evidence-first, expandable toward a future live dashboard. No invented counts.

### Partnership
Corporate and concise. Target government, companies, startups, investors, universities, communities, media, and senior entrepreneurs.

Do not label network/affiliation relationships as formal partnerships without documentation.

### Gallery
Editorial photography layout with varied image ratios, metadata, captions, and whitespace. Never a uniform thumbnail dump.

### Join
Strong minimal ending:

`Build business.`
`Grow network.`
`Create impact.`

Primary CTA: `Join HIPMI PT`
Secondary CTA: `Collaborate With Us`

- large tap targets
- clear locale control
- no tiny nested dropdown trees

## Copy direction

Tone:
- specific
- confident
- concise
- factual
- human
- institutionally mature

Avoid:
- vague aspiration
- startup jargon
- empty claims such as “revolutionizing the future”
- unexplained superlatives
- fabricated scale
- repetitive AI sentence patterns

Indonesian is canonical. English must be intentionally translated and reviewed.

## Anti-AI-slop rules

Reject a design if it contains:
- generic purple-blue gradients
- default centered SaaS hero
- meaningless floating cards
- random glassmorphism
- a grid of identical rounded cards for every section
- fake statistics
- fake partner logos
- low-resolution imagery hidden behind dark blur
- synthetic portraits presented as real people
- decorative 3D objects unrelated to HIPMI PT
- looping animation that competes with reading
- excessive glow
- generic icon rows used instead of actual information

## Phase boundary

Phase 2 masters assets/content. Phase 3 begins the production shell and flagship hero.
